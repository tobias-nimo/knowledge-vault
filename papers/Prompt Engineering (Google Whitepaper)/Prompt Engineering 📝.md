Prompt engineering is the process of designing high-quality prompts that guide
LLMs to produce accurate outputs.

This is an **iterative process** that involves tinkering with [[LLM Output Configuration]] and different [[Prompting Techniques]] until reaching the **desired output** length, style and structure in relation to the task.

> [[Best Practices]]: finding the right prompt requires tinkering, so play around with your prompts and try various models.
